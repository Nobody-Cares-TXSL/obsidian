# Lesson 33: Where is the Jones family?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/033&034－A%20Fine%20Day

## 原文

It is a fine day today.

There are some clouds in the sky, but the sun is shining.

Mr. Jones is with his family.

They are walking over the bridge.

There are some boats on the river.

Mr. Jones and his wife are looking at them.

Sally is looking at a big ship.

The ship is going under the bridge.

Tim is looking at an aeroplane.

The aeroplane is flying over the river.

## 句型结构

### "look at" 注视

> 短语动词，`look at + 名词/代词宾格`，表示"看……"。

- `Mr. Jones and his wife are looking at them.` — 琼斯先生和他的妻子正在看着它们
- `Sally is looking at a big ship.` — 莎莉正在看一艘很大的船
- `Tim is looking at an aeroplane.` — 蒂姆正在看一架飞机
- 宾语是代词时用宾格：`look at them`（不是 ~~look at they~~）

### "over" 空间介词

> 表示"在……上方（不接触）"或"越过"，与 [[Lesson 31 - Where's Sally]] 的 `under`（在……下面）形成反义对照。

- `walking over the bridge` — 走过桥
- `flying over the river` — 在河上空飞
