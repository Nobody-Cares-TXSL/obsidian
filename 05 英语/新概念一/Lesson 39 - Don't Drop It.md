# Lesson 39: Where does Sam put the vase in the end?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/039&040－Don't%20Drop%20It

## 原文

What are you going to do with that vase, Penny?

I'm going to put it on this table, Sam.

Don't do that.

Give it to me.

What are you going to do with it?

I'm going to put it here, in front of the window.

Be careful! Don't drop it!

Don't put it there, Sam.

Put it here, on this shelf.

There we are!

It's a lovely vase.

Those flowers are lovely, too.

## 句型结构

### "do with" — 处理搭配

> `What are you going to do with...?` 询问对某物的打算或处理方式

- `What are you going to do with that vase?` — 你打算怎么处理那个花瓶？
- `What are you going to do with it?` — 你打算怎么处理它？
- `do with` 是固定搭配，`with` 不能省略

### "give + 宾语 + to + 人" — 双宾语结构

> 当直接宾语是代词（it/them）时，结构变为 `give + it/them + to + 人`

- `Give it to me.` — 把它给我
- 名词作直接宾语可用两种语序：`Give me a book.` / `Give a book to me.` → 详见 [[Lesson 21 - Which book]]

**注意**：代词作直接宾语时只能用 `give it to me`，不能 ~~give me it~~

## 语法要点

### "in front of" — 方位介词短语

> 表示"在……前面"，描述某物在另一物前方

- `in front of the window` — 在窗子前面
- 与其他方位介词（on / in / beside / between）用法类似 → 详见 [[Lesson 25 - Mrs. Smith's Kitchen]]、[[Lesson 35 - Our Village]]
