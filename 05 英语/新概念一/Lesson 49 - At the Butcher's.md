# Lesson 49: What does Mr. Bird like?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/049&050－At%20the%20Butcher's

## 原文

Do you want any meat today, Mrs. Bird?

Yes, please.

Do you want beef or lamb?

Beef, please.

This lamb's very good.

I like lamb, but my husband doesn't.

What about some steak?

This is a nice piece.

Give me that piece, please.

And a pound of mince, too.

Do you want a chicken, Mrs. Bird?

They're very nice.

No, thank you.

My husband likes steak,

but he doesn't like chicken.

To tell you the truth, Mrs. Bird,

I don't like chicken either!

## 句型结构

### "Do you want A or B?" — 选择疑问句

> 用 or 连接两个选项，要求对方从中选一个，回答不再是 Yes/No 而是直接选一个

- `Do you want beef or lamb?` — 您想要牛肉还是羊肉？
- 回答直接选一个：`Beef, please.` / `Lamb, please.`
- 与一般疑问句的区别：一般疑问句用 Yes/No 回答，选择疑问句直接选选项

### "What about...?" — 提建议

> 固定搭配，用于提出建议或询问对方意见，相当于 "How about...?"

- `What about some steak?` — 来点牛排怎么样？
- 后面接名词或动名词（What about going...?）

## 语法要点

### doesn't — 第三人称单数否定

> 第三人称单数主语（he/she/it/人名）的否定句用 doesn't + 动词原形，不用 don't

- `my husband doesn't (like lamb)` — 我丈夫不喜欢（羊肉）
- `he doesn't like chicken` — 他不喜欢鸡肉
- doesn't = does not，does 是第三人称单数的助动词

**注意**：doesn't 后面的动词必须用原形，不能用 ~~he doesn't likes~~

### "To tell you the truth" — 插入语

> 用不定式短语作插入语，表示"说实话/坦白说"，放在句首用逗号隔开，删掉后句子依然完整

- `To tell you the truth, Mrs. Bird, I don't like chicken either!` — 说实话，伯德太太，我也不喜欢鸡肉！
- 类似插入语：To be honest（老实说）
