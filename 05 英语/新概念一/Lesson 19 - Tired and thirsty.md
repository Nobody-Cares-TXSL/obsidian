# Lesson 19: Why do the children thank their mother?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/019&020－Tired%20and%20Thirsty

## 原文

What's the matter, children?

We're tired ...

... and thirsty, Mum.

Sit down here.

Are you all right now?

No, we aren't.

Look!

There's an ice cream man.

Two ice creams, please.

Here you are, children.

Thanks, Mum.

These ice creams are nice.

Are you all right now?

Yes, we are, thank you!

## 句型结构

### What's the matter?

> 询问"怎么了/出什么事了"，用于关心对方状态，是固定搭配，**the matter** 不作"事情"解。

- 回答直接说情况：`We're tired and thirsty.`
- 也可说 `What's wrong?` 或 `What's the matter with you?`（后者语气更强，带指责感）

**注意**：不能说 "What's matter?" — 必须有 **the**

### Two [noun]s, please.

> 点单/购物时用 **数量 + 复数名词 + please**，简洁礼貌。

- `Two ice creams, please.` = 请来两份冰淇淋
- 不可数名词不能直接加数量：~~Two water~~ → `Two bottles of water, please.`

**注意**：可数名词要加复数 -s：`Two ice cream`s`，不是 "Two ice cream"

---

## 语法要点

### There is/are（存在句）

> 用 **There is/are** 表示"某处有某物"，结构为 **There + be + 名词 + 地点/场景**。

- 单数用 `There is`（缩写 `There's`），复数用 `There are`
- `There's an ice cream man.` = 有一个卖冰淇淋的（人）
- 与 have/has 不同：There is 表示"存在"，have 表示"拥有"

**注意**：`There's` 后面是真正的主语，所以 `There's an ice cream man` 不是 "There is a ice cream man"
