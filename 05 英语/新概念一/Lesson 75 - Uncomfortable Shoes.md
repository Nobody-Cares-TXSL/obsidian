# Lesson 75: What's wrong with the fashionable shoes?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/075&076－Uncomfortable%20Shoes

## 原文

Do you have any shoes like these?

What size?

Size five.

What colour?

Black.

I'm sorry.

We don't have any.

But my sister bought this pair last month.

Did she buy them here?

No, she bought them in the U.S.

We had some shoes like those a month ago, but we don't have any now.

Can you get a pair for me, please?

I'm afraid that I can't.

They were in fashion last year and the year before last.

But they're not in fashion this year.

These shoes are in fashion now.

They look very uncomfortable.

They are very uncomfortable.

But women always wear uncomfortable shoes!

## 句型结构

### I'm afraid (that)...

> **委婉表达**，用于礼貌地拒绝请求或传递坏消息，语气比直接说 No 缓和得多。

- `I'm afraid that I can't.` — 恐怕不行。
- that 可省略：`I'm afraid I can't.`
- 常接否定句或不利消息：`I'm afraid I don't know.` / `I'm afraid we don't have any.`
- 回应 [[Lesson 59 - Is That All]] 的 "Do you have any...?" 等请求时，用此句型拒绝比直接 No 更得体

**注意**：I'm afraid 不是"我害怕"的意思，这里是"恐怕/很遗憾"的委婉用法

## 语法要点

### in fashion / not in fashion

> **固定搭配**，表示"流行/过时"，描述事物是否符合当下潮流。

- `They were in fashion last year.` — 它们去年很流行。
- `They're not in fashion this year.` — 它们今年不流行了。
- 可用副词修饰：`These shoes are in fashion now.`（现在流行）
- 反义表达：`not in fashion` = `out of fashion`

### un- + 形容词

> **否定前缀 un-**，加在形容词前表示相反含义，是英语常见的派生构词法。

- `uncomfortable` = un- + comfortable → 不舒服的
- 其他常见例子：`happy` → `unhappy`、`lucky` → `unlucky`、`fair` → `unfair`
- un- 前缀不改变词性，形容词仍为形容词

**注意**：un- 只能加在形容词前变反义，不能加在动词前否定动作（否定动词用 don't/doesn't）
