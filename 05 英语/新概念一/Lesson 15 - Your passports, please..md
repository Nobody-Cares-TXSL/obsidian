# Lesson 15: Is there a problem with the Customs officer?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/015&016－Your%20Passports,%20Please%2E

## 原文

Are you Swedish?

No, we are not.

We are Danish.

Are your friends Danish too?

No, they aren't.

They are Norwegian.

Your passports, please.

Here they are.

Are these your cases?

No, they aren't.

Our cases are brown.

Here they are.

Are you tourists?

Yes, we are.

Are your friends tourists too?

Yes, they are.

That's fine.

Thank you very much.

## 句型结构

### Are these your [noun]?

> **these** 是 **this** 的复数形式，结构同 [[Lesson 11 - Is this your shirt]] 的 `Is this your [noun]?`，仅将单数改为复数。

- this → these，is → are，it → they：`Is this your case?` → `Are these your cases?`
- 回答也对应复数化：`Yes, they are.` / `No, they aren't.`

**注意**：不要说 "Are this your cases?" — 单数用 this/is，复数用 these/are

### Your [noun], please.

> 在服务场景中，**名词 + please** 表示礼貌请求，意为"请出示/请给我……"。

- `Your passports, please.` = 请出示护照
- 类似表达：`Your ticket, please.` / `Five tickets, please.`
