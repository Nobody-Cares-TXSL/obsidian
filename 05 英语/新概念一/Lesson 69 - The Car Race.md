# Lesson 69: Which car was the winner in 1995?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/069&070－The%20Car%20Race

## 原文

There is a car race near our town every year.

In 1995, there was a very big race.

There were hundreds of people there.

My wife and I were at the race.

Our friends Julie and Jack were there, too.

You can see us in the crowd.

We are standing on the left.

There were twenty cars in the race.

There were English cars, French cars, German cars, Italian cars, American cars and Japanese cars.

It was an exciting finish.

The winner was Billy Stewart.

He was in car number fifteen.

Five other cars were just behind him.

On the way home, my wife said to me, 'Don't drive so quickly!

You're not Billy Stewart!'

## 句型结构

### There was/were + 名词 + 地点

> **There be 句型的过去时形式**，用于描述过去某时存在的人或事物。

- 单数/不可数 → **There was**：`There was a very big race.`、`There was an exciting finish.`
- 复数 → **There were**：`There were hundreds of people there.`
- 规则与现在时一致，仅 be 动词变为 was/were

**注意**：不要受中文"有"影响写成 had。`There was` 表示"存在"，`had` 表示"拥有"

### Don't + 动词原形

> **否定祈使句**，表示禁止或劝阻，是 [[Lesson 13 - A new dress]] 肯定祈使句的否定形式。

- `Don't drive so quickly!` — 别开这么快！
- 直接 Don't + 动词原形，不加主语 you
- 口语中可加 please 缓和语气：`Don't drive so quickly, please.`

---

## 语法要点

### hundreds of + 复数名词

> **hundreds of** 表示"数百/成百上千"，用于描述不确定的大致数量。

- `hundreds of people`（数百人）、`hundreds of cars`（数百辆车）
- 结构：数字 + hundred + of + 复数名词（表示概数时 hundred 加 s）
- 表示确切数字时 hundred 不加 s：`two hundred people`（两百人）

**注意**：表示概数加 s 和 of，表示确切数字不加 s 不加 of
