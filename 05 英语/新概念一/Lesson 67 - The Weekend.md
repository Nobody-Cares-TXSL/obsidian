# Lesson 67: What are the Johnsons going to do at the weekend?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/067&068－The%20Weekend

## 原文

Hello. Were you at the butcher's?

Yes, I was.

Were you at the butcher's, too?

No, I wasn't.

I was at the greengrocer's.

How's Jimmy today?

He's very well, thank you.

Was he absent from school last week?

Yes, he was.

He was absent on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

How are you all keeping?

Very well, thank you.

We're going to spend three days in the country!

We're going to stay at my mother's for the weekend.

Friday, Saturday and Sunday in the country!

Aren't you lucky!

## 句型结构

### Were you at [place]?

> **be 动词一般过去时**的疑问句形式，用于询问过去某时的情况或状态。

- `Were you at the butcher's?` / `Was he absent from school last week?`
- 肯定回答：`Yes, I was.` / `Yes, he was.`
- 否定回答：`No, I wasn't.`
- I/he/she/it → **was**，you/we/they → **were**

**注意**：过去时疑问句直接将 was/were 提前，**不需要**助动词 did

---

## 语法要点

### be 动词一般过去时 was/were

> be 动词的过去式，表示过去的状态或事实，不强调动作过程。

| 主语 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| I/he/she/it | was | was not (wasn't) | Was...? |
| you/we/they | were | were not (weren't) | Were...? |

- `I was at the greengrocer's.`（我在蔬菜店）
- `He was absent on Monday.`（他周一缺课了）
- was/were 不随人称变化（只分两组），与 am/is/are 的三组对应

### "'s + 人名/职业" 表示店铺

> 在人名或职业后加 `'s`，表示"……的店"，是 [[Lesson 11 - Is this your shirt]] 名词所有格的延伸用法。

- `the butcher's` = the butcher's shop（肉店）
- `the greengrocer's`（蔬菜水果店）、`the hairdresser's`（理发店）、`the dentist's`（牙医诊所）
- `my mother's` = my mother's house（妈妈家），也可表示住所

### spend + 时间段 + in/at 地点

> spend 后接时间段再接地点，表示在某地度过多少时间。

- `spend three days in the country`（在乡下待三天）
- `spend the weekend at my mother's`（在妈妈家过周末）
- 也可接 doing：`spend the weekend reading`

### Aren't you lucky!

> **否定疑问句**可用于表示感叹或惊讶，预期回答是肯定的。

- `Aren't you lucky!`（你真幸运！）— 不是真问，是感叹
- `Isn't it beautiful!`（真漂亮啊！）
- 结构：`Aren't/Isn't + 主语 + 形容词/名词 + !`
