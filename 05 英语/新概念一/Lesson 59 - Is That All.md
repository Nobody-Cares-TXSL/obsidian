# Lesson 59: Does the lady buy any chalk?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/059&060－Is%20That%20All

## 原文

I want some envelopes, please.

Do you want the large size or the small size?

The large size, please.

Do you have any writing paper?

Yes, we do.

I don't have any small pads.

I only have large ones.

Do you want a pad?

Yes, please.

And I want some glue.

A bottle of glue.

And I want a large box of chalk, too.

I only have small boxes.

Do you want one?

No, thank you.

Is that all?

That's all, thank you.

What else do you want?

I want my change.

## 句型结构

### "What else...?" — 还有什么？

> 疑问词（what/who/where 等）后面加 else，表示"还有什么/谁/哪里"

- `What else do you want?` — 你还想要什么？
- else 固定放在疑问词**之后**（what else, who else, where else）
- 回答 What else? 时可直接说出具体物品

**注意**：else 不与 which 搭配（没有 which else 的用法）
