# Lesson 61: What is good news for Jimmy?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/061&062－A%20Bad%20Cold

## 原文

Where's Jimmy?

He's in bed.

What's the matter with him?

He feels ill.

He looks ill.

We must call the doctor.

Yes, we must.

Can you remember the doctor's telephone number?

Yes. It's 09754.

Open your mouth, Jimmy.

Show me your tongue. Say, 'Ah'.

What's the matter with him, doctor?

He has a bad cold, Mr. Williams, so he must stay in bed for a week.

That's good news for Jimmy.

Good news? Why?

Because he doesn't like school!

## 句型结构

### "have a + 疾病名" — 表达患病

> 用 have + a + 疾病名词表达"得了……病"

- `He has a bad cold.` — 他得了重感冒。
- 常见搭配：have a cold（感冒）、have a fever（发烧）、have a headache（头痛）
- 可用 bad 修饰程度：have a bad cold（重感冒）

**注意**：疾病名是可数名词时用 a（a cold），不可数名词时不用（have flu），但中文常统称"感冒"

---

## 语法要点

### "feel / look + 形容词" — 感官系动词

> feel 和 look 是感官系动词，后接形容词描述主语的状态或外观，不用副词

- `He feels ill.` — 他感觉不舒服。（描述自身感受）
- `He looks ill.` — 他看起来不舒服。（描述他人外观）
- 常见搭配：feel tired/happy/well、look tired/happy/well

**注意**：系动词后必须用形容词，不能说 He feels illly / He looks illly

### "for + 时间段" — 持续时间

> for + 时间段表示动作或状态持续多久，回答 how long 的问题

- `stay in bed for a week` — 卧床一周
- `for a week / for two days / for three months`
- 常与 must、can't 等情态动词连用：`must stay in bed for a week`

**注意**：for + 时间段（for a week）区别于 in + 时间点（in a week 一周后）
