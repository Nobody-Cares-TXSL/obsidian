# Lesson 43: How do you know Sam doesn't make the tea very often?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/043&044－Hurry%20Up

## 原文

Can you make the tea, Sam?

Yes, of course I can, Penny.

Is there any water in this kettle?

Yes, there is.

Where's the tea?

It's over there, behind the teapot.

Can you see it?

I can see the teapot, but I can't see the tea.

There it is!

It's in front of you!

Ah yes, I can see it now.

Where are the cups?

There are some in the cupboard.

Can you find them?

Yes. Here they are.

Hurry up, Sam!

The kettle's boiling!

## 句型结构

### "Can you + 动词原形?" — 礼貌请求

> 用情态动词 can 询问对方能力或请求对方做某事，语气比直接祈使句更礼貌

- `Can you make the tea?` — 你能泡茶吗？（请求）
- `Can you see it?` — 你能看到它吗？（询问能力）
- `Can you find them?` — 你能找到它们吗？（请求）
- 肯定回答：`Yes, of course I can.`（of course 加强肯定语气）
- 否定回答：`I can't see the tea.`（can't = can not）

**注意**：can 后面必须接动词原形，不能用 ~~can you to make~~ 或 ~~can you makes~~

### "Hurry up!" — 催促

> 固定搭配，用于催促他人加快速度

- `Hurry up, Sam!` — 快点，萨姆！
- 可接人名作呼语，也可单独使用

## 语法要点

### behind — 方位介词

> behind 表示"在……后面"，与已学的 [[Lesson 39 - Don't Drop It|in front of]]（在……前面）互为反义词

- `behind the teapot` — 在茶壶后面
- 同类方位介词：[[Lesson 35 - Our Village|beside]]（旁边）、[[Lesson 27 - Mrs. Smith's Living Room|near]]（附近）、between（之间）

**注意**：behind 是单个介词，而 in front of 是短语介词，不能省略 of（~~in front you~~）

### any 在疑问句 / some 在肯定句

> 疑问句中用 any、肯定句中用 some 表示"一些"，修饰不可数名词或复数名词

- `Is there any water...?` — 有水吗？（疑问句用 any）
- `There are some in the cupboard.` — 橱柜里有几个。（肯定句用 some）
- some 的用法详见 [[Lesson 27 - Mrs. Smith's Living Room]]

**注意**：any 也可用于否定句（如 I don't have any time），不可用于肯定句（~~I have any books~~）
