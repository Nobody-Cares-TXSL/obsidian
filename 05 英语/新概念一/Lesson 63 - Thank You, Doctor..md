# Lesson 63: Who else is in bed today? Why?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/063&064－Thank%20You%2C%20Doctor%2E

## 原文

How's Jimmy today?

Better.

Thank you, doctor.

Can I see him please, Mrs. Williams?

Certainly, doctor.

Come upstairs.

You look very well, Jimmy.

You are better now, but you mustn't get up yet.

You must stay in bed for another two days.

The boy mustn't go to school yet, Mrs. Williams.

And he mustn't eat rich food.

Does he have a temperature, doctor?

No, he doesn't.

Must he stay in bed?

Yes. He must remain in bed for another two days.

He can get up for about two hours each day, but you must keep the room warm.

Where's Mr. Williams this evening?

He's in bed, doctor.

Can you see him please?

He has a bad cold, too!

## 句型结构

### "mustn't + 动词原形" — 禁止

> must 的否定形式 mustn't 表示禁止或不要做某事，语气强烈

- `You mustn't get up yet.` — 你还不能起床。
- `He mustn't go to school yet.` — 他还不能去上学。
- `He mustn't eat rich food.` — 他不能吃油腻的食物。
- mustn't = must not，表示说话人的禁止，不是"不需要"

**注意**：mustn't（禁止）与 don't have to / needn't（不必）含义不同。`You mustn't go`（不许去） vs. `You don't have to go`（可以不去）

---

## 语法要点

### "keep + 宾语 + 形容词" — 使保持某种状态

> keep 后接宾语再加形容词，表示使某人/某物保持某种状态

- `keep the room warm` — 保持房间温暖
- 常见搭配：keep the door open、keep your hands clean、keep the room tidy
- keep 在此作使役动词，结构为 keep + 宾语 + 宾补（形容词）

### "for another + 数量" — 再持续……

> for another + 时间段，表示在原有基础上再延续一段时间

- `for another two days` — 再（卧床）两天
- `for another two hours` — 再两个小时
- another 此处表示"再、又"，不是"另一个"
