# Lesson 65: Does Jill take the key to the front door?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/065&066－Not%20a%20Baby

## 原文

What are you going to do this evening, Jill?

I'm going to meet some friends, Dad.

You mustn't come home late.

You must be home at half past ten.

I can't get home so early, Dad!

Can I have the key to the front door, please?

No, you can't.

Jill's eighteen years old, Tom.

She's not a baby.

Give her the key.

She always comes home early.

Oh, all right!

Here you are.

But you mustn't come home after a quarter past eleven.

Do you hear?

Yes, Dad.

Thanks, Mum.

That's all right.

Goodbye.

Enjoy yourself!

We always enjoy ourselves, Mum.

Bye-bye.

## 句型结构

### Enjoy yourself!

> **反身代词**用于表示动作回到主语本身，`enjoy + 反身代词` 表示"玩得开心/享受自己"。

- 主语为 you → yourself，主语为 we → ourselves
- 反身代词形式：myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves
- 常见搭配：`enjoy oneself`、`help oneself to`、`behave oneself`

**注意**：enjoy 后接反身代词表示"玩得开心"，接名词含义不同：`enjoy the party`（享受派对）

## 语法要点

### the key to the front door

> **名词 + to + 名词** 结构表示"……的……"，用于描述事物之间的关联或归属。

- `the key to the door`（门的钥匙）、`the answer to the question`（问题的答案）、`the way to school`（去学校的路）
- to 在这里是介词，表示"对应/通往"，不是不定式
- 常见于 key, answer, solution, entrance, way 等名词后
