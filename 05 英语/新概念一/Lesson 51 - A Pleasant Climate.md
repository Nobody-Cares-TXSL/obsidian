# Lesson 51: Does it ever snow in Greece?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/051&052－A%20Pleasant%20Climate

## 原文

Where do you come from?

I come from Greece.

What's the climate like in your country?

It's very pleasant.

What's the weather like in spring?

It's often windy in March.

It's always warm in April and May, but it rains sometimes.

What's it like in summer?

It's always hot in June, July and August.

The sun shines every day.

Is it cold or warm in autumn?

It's always warm in September and October.

It's often cold in November and it rains sometimes.

Is it very cold in winter?

It's often cold in December, January and February.

It snows sometimes.

## 句型结构

### "Where do you come from?" — 询问来源地

> 用 come from 固定搭配询问对方来自哪里/是哪里人

- `I come from Greece.` — 我来自希腊。
- come from 是固定搭配，表示"来自"，不能拆开理解
- `Where do you come from?` = 你是哪里人？（询问籍贯）

### "What's ... like?" — 询问特征/情况

> 固定句型，用 "What's + 主语 + like?" 询问某事物的特征或情况，like 在此是介词"像"，不是动词"喜欢"

- `What's the climate like in your country?` — 你们国家的气候怎么样？
- `What's the weather like in spring?` — 春天的天气是怎样的？
- 简略形式：`What's it like in summer?`（省略了 the weather）

**注意**：like 在此是介词，不是动词"喜欢"。判定方法：like 前面有系动词 be，后面跟疑问词 what 作宾语

---

## 语法要点

### 频率副词 always / often / sometimes

> 频率副词描述动作发生的频率，位于 be 动词之后、实义动词之前

- always（总是，100%）→ `It's always warm.` / `It always rains.`
- often（经常，>50%）→ `It's often windy.`
- sometimes（有时候，<50%）→ `it rains sometimes.`
- sometimes 位置灵活，可放在句首、句中或句末

**注意**：频率递减顺序为 always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never

### "It + 天气动词/形容词" — 描述天气

> 英语描述天气时固定用 it 作形式主语，it 不指代任何具体事物

- `It's warm / hot / cold / windy.` — 天暖/热/冷/有风。（be 动词 + 形容词）
- `It rains / snows.` — 下雨/下雪。（it + 天气动词）
- 中文描述天气不需要主语（"下雨了"、"天很暖"），翻译时去掉 it
