# Lesson 25: What colour is the electric cooker?
> https://nce.luzhenhua.cn/lesson.html#NCE1/025&026－Mrs.%20Smith's%20Kitchen

## 原文

Mrs. Smith's kitchen is small.

There is a refrigerator in the kitchen.

The refrigerator is white.

It is on the right.

There is an electric cooker in the kitchen.

The cooker is blue.

It is on the left.

There is a table in the middle of the room.

There is a bottle on the table.

The bottle is empty.

There is a cup on the table, too.

The cup is clean.

## 句型结构

### 方位介词：on the right / on the left / in the middle of

> 用介词短语描述物品在空间中的位置关系。

- `on the right` 在右边：`It is on the right.`
- `on the left` 在左边：`It is on the left.`
- `in the middle of` 在……中间：`in the middle of the room`

**注意**：`in the middle of` 后面要加定冠词 `the`，不能说 ~~in middle of~~

---

## 语法要点

### There is + 名词 + 方位介词短语

> 在 [[Lesson 19 - Tired and thirsty]] 讲过的 There is/are 存在句基础上，用方位介词短语精确描述物品位置。

- `There is a refrigerator in the kitchen.` — 厨房里有一台冰箱
- `There is a table in the middle of the room.` — 房间中间有一张桌子
- `There is a bottle on the table.` — 桌子上有一个瓶子
